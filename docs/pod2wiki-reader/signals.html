Jason Lemkin / @jasonlk / engagement 110

Why is traditional “SaaS” dead? Well B2B software isn’t. It’s growing faster than ever. But as trite as it sounds, SaaS from 2005-2024 or so really was “Building Software That Doesn’t Suck” You took older software, made a much easier / slicker version for humans to use, often with better integrations and simpler workflows … and that was is it. That was the playbook. That was the playbook. E.g., HubSpot CRM alone was just that, and now it’s their largest business. An easier to use , for the time, slicker version of Salesforce CRM. That alone also took just a few months to get to a v1 maybe, but 2-3 years to really nail pre-agentic coding. Years to keep iterating and just get to feature parity, one quarterly release at a time. AI didn’t kill B2B software. But it made “build XYZ that doesn’t suck” sort of a dead value proposition. That still matters. But it’s just not enough today. And the pace of development is so much faster, it’s not even a distinguishing factor anymore. Now it’s about using agents and AI to make a category wildly more efficient, productive, and effective.
