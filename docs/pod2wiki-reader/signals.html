Jason Lemkin / @jasonlk / engagement 51

In the old days of B2B (circa 2006-2024), my general advice was don't figure it all out on TAM in the early days. Get to $1m ARR first however you can. Then after that, to $10m ARR. Great founders that get to $10m ARR almost always unlocked more TAM. The markets almost always dragged you there and showed you the way, anyway. That's not how I see things in a 2x ARR world. When software reaches a slow growth state, it's worth 2x ARR today. Yes, sometimes much more if your free cash flow is massive (e.g., Zoom). But so many are worth 2x ARR now once growth slows. So now I push founders much harder here. You need to capture AI budget. You need to find the huge TAM unlock earlier. Not on Day 1. But much earlier. In the Age of AI, we see the penalty for slowing growth more accutely than ever in our B2B lifetimes. So you have to unlock and attack massive TAMs early. And you really have to grab the budget. Today, that's for AI / agents. Everything else is getting cut and rationalized. Is your TTAM big enough? Your True TAM? And did it at least double this year? It has to at least double every year. Ideally, much more than that. But at least double.
